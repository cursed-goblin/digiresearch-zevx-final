Our Method

Research first.
Then spend.

Most small businesses lose money on marketing because nobody checked the basics first. Our whole method exists to remove guesswork before the money goes out.

The four phases

Every project runs the same way.

01

Research

We study your market, your competitors' ads and pages, what your buyers search for, and where your current setup loses them.

  • Competitor ad and landing page teardown
  • Keyword and demand check
  • Audience and offer notes
02

Plan

You get one page: what we will do, in what order, what it costs, and the target we are aiming at. No jargon.

  • Written scope and one honest price
  • Target cost per enquiry
  • Timeline with dates
03

Build and launch

The website goes live or the campaigns go on. Tracking is set up first, so we can see results from day one.

  • Tracking before spending
  • A staged preview for your review
  • Launch checklist before going live
04

Measure and improve

Weekly numbers, an honest read of them, and one clear change we recommend for next week.

  • Weekly report in plain language
  • What we changed and why
  • Next week's recommendation

The free audit

What you get before paying anything.

Twenty minutes on a call or a WhatsApp chat, and a written summary you can keep — even if you never work with us.

In the audit

  • A read of your website: speed, structure, mobile
  • A read of your ads, if you run any
  • Three competitor examples worth learning from
  • The three fixes we would do first
  • An honest yes or no on whether we can help

What we need from you

  • Your website link, if you have one
  • What you sell, and to whom
  • Your rough monthly marketing budget
  • Twenty minutes to talk or chat

Typical first project

About ten days, start to live.

1

Day 1–2 · Research

We look at your market, competitors and current setup, and write down what we find.

2

Day 3 · Plan and quote

You approve a one-page plan with scope, price and target before anything is built.

3

Day 4–9 · Build and review

We build, you review on a private link, and we fix what you flag.

4

Day 10 · Launch

We go live, check tracking and forms, and hand over with a short walkthrough.
